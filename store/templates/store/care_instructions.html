Jewelry Care Instructions

Keep your precious pieces looking beautiful for years to come

General Care Tips

  • Remove jewelry before swimming, showering, or exercising
  • Store pieces separately to avoid scratching
  • Clean regularly with a soft, lint-free cloth
  • Avoid contact with perfumes, lotions, and chemicals

Artificial / Fashion Jewellery

General Care

Wipe pieces gently with a soft, dry cloth after every use to remove sweat, oils, and dust. Avoid soaking in water or using strong cleaning agents, as this can damage the plating and stones.

cleaning

If needed, clean lightly with a damp cloth dipped in mild soapy water, then dry completely before storing. Do not scrub or immerse artificial jewellery, as adhesives may loosen.

storage

Keep each piece separately in airtight pouches or boxes to prevent scratches and fading. Avoid contact with perfumes, sprays, and direct sunlight for longer-lasting shine.

Pro Tip

For heirloom pieces or valuable jewelry, consider professional cleaning and inspection at least once a year to maintain their beauty and integrity.
